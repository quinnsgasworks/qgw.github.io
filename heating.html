Cold radiators? Noisy system?

If your radiators are cold at the bottom, your system is making strange banging or gurgling noises, or your heating is taking ages to warm up, your system might be full of sludge. A powerflush can make a huge difference, and we'll let you know honestly if it's needed or not.

Thinking about a smart thermostat? They can knock a noticeable amount off your gas bills and let you control your heating from your phone. Ask us about Hive, Nest and Tado.
