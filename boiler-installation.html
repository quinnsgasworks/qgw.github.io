New Boiler Installation

Whether you're upgrading an old boiler or fitting one for the first time, we'll help you choose the right model, install it cleanly and certify it, all across Greater Manchester.

Why upgrade your boiler?

  • A-rated efficiency, lower gas bills
  • Quieter, more reliable heating and hot water
  • Manufacturer warranty (often up to 10 years)
  • Smart thermostat compatibility
  • Peace of mind that everything is safe and certified

What's included in the install

  • Free no-pressure quote and survey
  • Removal and safe disposal of your old boiler
  • Installation of your new boiler and any associated pipework
  • Full commissioning, testing and Gas Safe certificate
  • Manufacturer warranty registration on your behalf

Boiler types we install

Combi, system and conventional boilers from trusted brands like Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi and Ideal. Not sure what's right for your home? We'll talk it through with you in plain English.

Thinking about a new boiler? Have a chat with us first, we'll give you honest advice on whether you actually need one and what would suit your home best.
