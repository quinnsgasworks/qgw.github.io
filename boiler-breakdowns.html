Boiler Breakdowns & Repairs

When your boiler stops working it can leave you without heating or hot water, sometimes at the worst possible time. We get you back up and running fast, across Manchester and Bolton.

Common boiler problems we fix

  • No heating or no hot water
  • Boiler leaking water
  • Strange noises (banging, whistling, kettling)
  • Pilot light keeps going out
  • Error codes on the display
  • Low pressure that won't hold
  • Radiators not heating properly
  • Frozen condensate pipes in winter

How it works

  • Quick phone or WhatsApp chat to understand the issue
  • Call-out and full diagnostic on your boiler
  • Honest, upfront quote before any work starts
  • Repair carried out and tested
  • Boiler left clean, safe and back in service

Brands we work with

We're confident on all the major UK boiler brands, including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Baxi, Ideal, Glow-worm, Viessmann, Potterton, Main and Alpha.
