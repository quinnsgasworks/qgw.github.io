Landlord Gas Safety Checks (CP12)

If you let property in Greater Manchester, you're legally required to have a Gas Safety Record carried out every 12 months. We make it quick, tidy and stress-free.

Book your CP12

What's checked

  • Visual inspection of every gas appliance in the property
  • Gas tightness test on the supply
  • Operating pressure and flue performance
  • Safety devices on each appliance
  • Carbon monoxide check
  • Issue of your CP12 / Landlord Gas Safety Record

Your legal responsibilities as a landlord

  • An annual gas safety check on all gas appliances and flues
  • Use of a Gas Safe registered engineer (that's us, Reg. 979693)
  • Provide a copy of the certificate to existing tenants within 28 days
  • Provide a copy to new tenants before they move in
  • Keep records for at least 2 years
