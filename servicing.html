Annual Boiler Servicing

An annual service keeps your boiler safe, efficient and reliable, and helps your warranty stay valid. Quick, tidy, and done by a Gas Safe registered engineer.

Book a service

What's included in a service

  • Full visual inspection of the boiler and flue
  • Check of gas pressure and flow
  • Combustion analysis to make sure it's burning cleanly
  • Test of all safety devices
  • Check of internal components and seals
  • Cleaning of key parts where needed
  • Service report for your records (and your warranty)

Why bother with an annual service?

  • Safety, catches issues like carbon monoxide leaks early
  • Warranty, most manufacturers require a yearly service to keep cover valid
  • Efficiency, a clean, well-tuned boiler uses less gas
  • Reliability, small problems get spotted before they become breakdowns

How often should I have it done?

Once a year is the standard recommendation. Many people book a service in late summer or early autumn, before the heating season really kicks in.
